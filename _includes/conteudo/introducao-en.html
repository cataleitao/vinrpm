RPM – Natural Wines & Spirits brings together two couples — José Roseira and Catarina Leitão, Daniel Pires and Marianne Baillot — around an old Quinta in the lower Tâmega, ten kilometres from the river Douro, in the transition zone between the Vinho Verde and Douro wine regions.

On a south-facing slope, sheltered from the north winds and nestled between watercourses, the granite terroir drinks from the mineral richness of the Verín-Chaves-Vidago hydrothermal system, which rises from a spring on a property adjacent to the Quinta. On the higher ground, vines more than sixty years old bring together several native varieties, Arinto chief among them. Our wines are foot-trodden and macerated in 250-year-old granite lagares, then fermented in concrete tanks with minimal intervention — always guided by the aim of conveying the singular character of their grapes and their terroir.
